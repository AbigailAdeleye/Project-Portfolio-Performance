## **Overview**

The report provides a consolidated view of project performance across schedule, cost, resources, risk, and business value.
The dashboard enables leadership to monitor overall portfolio health, identify underperforming programmes and projects, understand the drivers of performance gaps, and prioritize areas requiring intervention.

The report is structured into three analytical areas:

- **Portfolio Performance** – Overall portfolio health, progress, schedule and cost performance.
- **Performance Drivers** – Programme, business unit, location, strategic theme and resource performance.
- **Risk & Action** – Project risks, mitigation actions, benefit realization and expected value.

The portfolio contains **97 projects**, with an overall **Portfolio Health Score of 90.12** and **81.47% actual progress**. However, progress is **4.89 percentage points below plan**, indicating that while the portfolio is generally healthy, delivery challenges exist.


## **Problem Statement**

Managing a large project portfolio makes it difficult for leadership to quickly identify:

- Which projects are falling behind schedule.
- Which programmes are driving poor portfolio performance.
- Where cost overruns are occurring.
- Whether resource constraints are affecting delivery.
- Which projects carry the highest risk.
- Whether projects are delivering their expected business benefits.

The dashboard was developed to bring these indicators into a single interactive view, helping decision-makers move from identifying performance issues to understanding their drivers and taking targeted action.

### *Methodology*

The analysis evaluates portfolio performance across five key dimensions:

1. **Schedule Performance**
   - Actual Progress %
   - Progress Variance %
   - SPI
   - Forecast Delay Days
   - Projects Behind Schedule

2. **Cost Performance**
   - Budget Variance %
   - CPI
   - Forecast Total Cost
   - Projects Over Cost
   - Forecast Cost Overruns

3. **Resource Performance**
   - Planned Resource Hours
   - Resource Capacity Gaps
   - Progress Variance

4. **Risk Performance**
   - Risk Exposure
   - Open Risks
   - Critical Risks
   - RAG Status
   - Overdue Mitigation Actions

5. **Business Value**
   - Forecast Benefit
   - Benefit Realisation %
   - Forecast Benefit relative to Budget

## **Key Insights**

- **Portfolio delivery is behind plan:** 44 projects are behind schedule, with actual progress at **81.47%** and progress variance at **-4.89%**.

- **Programme performance varies significantly:** Finance Transformation has the largest progress gap at **-14.71%**, while Data & AI Enablement is ahead of plan at **+1.82%**.

- **Resource constraints are widespread:** **50 projects** have identified resource gaps, creating potential delivery constraints.

- **Some projects have severe cost and schedule issues:** Cloud Enablement 055 records progress variance below **-26%**, alongside weak SPI/CPI and significant budget variance.

- **Risk actions require attention:** The portfolio has **185 overdue mitigation actions**, alongside open and critical risks.

- **Business value realization is low:** Forecast benefit is **€148M**, while overall benefit realization is only **44.57%**. A further **31 healthy projects** have low benefit realization.

---

## **Business Recommendations**

- **Launch recovery plans for severely delayed projects:** Require project owners to provide root-cause analysis, revised timelines and clear recovery actions.

- **Reallocate resources to priority projects:** Review the **50 projects with resource gaps** and prioritize available resources toward strategically important projects facing delivery constraints.

- **Escalate projects with combined cost and schedule problems:** Place projects with poor SPI and CPI under leadership review until corrective actions improve performance.

- **Focus intervention on underperforming programmes:** Conduct targeted reviews of **Finance Transformation and Regulatory Readiness** to identify and remove the causes of their delivery gaps.

- **Clear overdue risk actions:** Assign owners and deadlines to the **185 overdue mitigation actions**, prioritizing critical risks.

- **Improve benefit realization:** Require the **31 healthy but low-benefit projects** to identify the reasons for the benefit gap and define actions to improve business outcomes.

- **Use the dashboard to drive decisions:** Incorporate the dashboard into monthly portfolio reviews, with flagged projects assigned a clear decision such as **recover, reallocate, re-scope, escalate or continue**.

## **Conclusion**

The portfolio is **generally healthy but requires targeted intervention**.

While the overall Portfolio Health Score is **90.12**, the analysis highlights significant schedule delays, resource gaps, project-level cost pressures, overdue risk actions and low benefit realization.

The dashboard therefore helps leadership move beyond simply asking **"Are our projects performing?"** to identifying **where performance is deteriorating, why it is happening, and where management action should be prioritized.**
